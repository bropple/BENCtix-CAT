With the lid closed
- The outer screen lights for a few seconds when the lid closes, when a notification arrives, and at the side or volume key: the time, date, battery, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the newest notification, over its wallpaper (Settings, Outer screen; a GIF moves).
Side key	Wake the outer screen; pressed again, the next notification.
Side key twice	Play or pause the music (or a video in the browser).
Side key held	Dismiss the notification shown.
Side + Volume down	The flashlight on or off.
Volume	Media volume, 0 to 15 as everywhere else.
Volume held	Next or previous song.
- While music or a video plays, the outer screen shows its title and artist under the date (grey when paused).
- Calls ring as usual; opening the lid answers.
