Notifications and quick settings
# Notifications (side key)
Up, Down	Choose one.
OK	Open it in its app (and dismiss it).
C	Dismiss it.
#	Dismiss them all.
Back, side key	Close the list.
- New notifications drop in at the top as a banner; tap it to open the list.

# Music in the list
- While a song is loaded, its card heads the list, chosen when the list opens: the cover, title, artist, the buttons and where the song is.
OK	Play or pause.
C	Stop (the card goes; the queue stays in Music).
Left, Right	The song before or after; held, scrub back or ahead.
Down	To the notifications (Up from the first one comes back).
- Tap the buttons, or the bar to jump there.
- Dismissing only removes the alert: the message stays in its app.

# Quick settings (side key twice)
Arrows	Move between the tiles.
OK	Switch the tile.
Left, Right	On the brightness bar: dimmer or brighter.
- Tiles: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Cellular (the radio), Mobile data, Sound (on, vibrate only, silent), Flashlight and Mullvad VPN.

# On the lock screen
- The side key opens notifications and quick settings there too.
- Opening a notification asks for the PIN first, then opens it.
- Cellular, Mobile data and Mullvad show a padlock: they only switch when unlocked.
