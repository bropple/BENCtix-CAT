Getting around
# Keys that work everywhere
Home	The home screen. Apps close as you leave them; the Shell keeps running.
Recents	The app you used before.
Back	Back a step. On an app's first page, it closes the app.
End	Ends or declines a call, and wakes the screen. Held for a second: the power menu (power off, reboot, reboot to lk2nd).
Call	Answers a call. In Phone it dials; elsewhere it does nothing.
Side key	Notifications. Pressed again: quick settings. Again: closed. Held: the flashlight, even with the screen off; with the lid shut, side + Volume down.
Volume	Media volume. During a call, or with Phone in front: the call volume. While a call rings: silence it.
End + Vol Down	A screenshot, saved to Pictures/screenshots. Photos shows them.
Speaker key	During a call: speakerphone on or off.
Lid	Closing it turns the screen off (and locks, with a PIN set). Opening it wakes the phone. With a call ringing, opening answers and closing declines.

# The screen
- Tap to choose, drag to scroll. Most lists work with a finger too.
- The bar at the bottom says what Back and OK do on that page.
- The status bar at the top: Wi-Fi, cellular, Bluetooth, the location arrow while GPS runs (grey while it searches, white with a fix), the vibrate or silent icon, the clock and the battery. While you type, it shows the T9 helper in the middle.
- The screen turns off after the screen timeout (Settings, Display). Any key wakes it; that key does nothing else.
