Photos
# The grid
Arrows	Move.
OK	Open a photo or wallpaper.
*	The other tab: Photos, Wallpapers.
Back	Close Photos.
- Photos: everything in Pictures and its folders, newest first: the camera's photos and your screenshots.
- Wallpapers: the pictures in Wallpapers/inner (home and lock screens) and Wallpapers/outer (the outer screen, marked "outer") on the memory card. PNG, JPEG, GIF and more.

# A photo
Left, Right	The photo before or after (or swipe).
Up, Down	Zoom: 1x, 2x, 4x.
2 4 6 8	Move around a zoomed photo (or drag).
OK	Move it to the wallpapers: for the home and lock screens, or the outer screen.
#	Its details.
C	Delete it (OK to confirm).
Back	Back to the grid.

# A wallpaper
- Shown as it will look: on the home screen, or on the outer screen (three times the size, moving at the speed set in Settings, Outer screen).
Left, Right	The one before or after.
OK	Set it: home screen, lock screen or both; or the outer screen.
C	Delete it (OK to confirm).
Back	Back to the grid.
- From Settings (Display, Outer screen) Photos shows that screen's wallpapers only: OK sets one, * sets none, and Photos closes.
