Settings
Up, Down	Choose a row.
OK, Left, Right	Change it, or open its page.
Back	Up a page; from the first page, close Settings.

# Pages
- A saved Wi-Fi network's page has Private address: the network sees an address of its own instead of this phone's (the same one each time you join it). Networks without a password, the public ones, get it when you add them; your own keep the phone's address. Turning it on or off joins again, with a new IP.
- Network and internet: Wi-Fi (scan, connect, forget), the mobile network (cellular on or off, mobile data, a test), VPN (Tailscale and Mullvad logins; Tailscale paused while the lid is closed, so the battery lasts longer, unless set to stay connected), and File sharing (see that topic).
- Connected devices: Bluetooth (pair, connect, forget). A code to compare is shown here during pairing.
- Display: sleep with the lid closed (on: the phone sleeps while the lid is closed and nothing plays; a call, a text, a key or opening the lid wake it).
- Display, and the outer screen: wallpapers for the home screen, the lock screen and the outer screen, chosen in Photos' Wallpapers tab (see Photos), and how fast a moving one plays on the outer screen.
- Sounds: sounds on or vibrate only, volumes, ringtone, notification sounds per app.
- Vibration: strength, patterns, and your own (hold OK in the rhythm you want).
- Security: the PIN lock.
- Accessibility: speech. Say who is calling (between rings) and read texts aloud (who it is from, then the text), at the ring volume and not while sounds are off. Voice: BENCmouth's voices, each heard as you choose it; Hear the voice plays a sample.
- About, and Developer options (the call microphone test).
- Passwords, keys and account numbers show as stars as you type.
