Lock screen
Digits	Type the PIN (shown as dots). It unlocks as soon as the PIN is right.
C	Delete a digit.
OK	Unlock (or try the PIN typed).
End held	Power off or reboot.
Side key	Notifications and quick settings.

- After 5 wrong PINs it waits 30 seconds, then twice as long after each further mistake.
- Settings, Security sets, changes or removes the PIN, and how soon the phone locks after the screen goes dark.
- A ringing or ongoing call shows over the lock screen.
- Its wallpaper is chosen in Settings, Display (none while music shows its cover).

# Music on the lock screen
- While a song is loaded, its cover fills the background and its card shows under the date.
OK	Play or pause, until a digit of the PIN is typed (then OK unlocks).
Left, Right	The song before or after; held, scrub back or ahead.
- Tap the card's buttons, or its bar to jump there.
