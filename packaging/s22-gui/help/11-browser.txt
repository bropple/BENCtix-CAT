Browser
# Browsing
Arrows	Move between links and fields; scroll where there are none.
OK	Follow the chosen link, or press the button.
2, 8	A screen up or down.
0	The top of the page.
4, 6	Back or forward.
5	Reload.
1, 3	Smaller or larger text.
C	Stop loading.
#	Type an address or a search.
*	The menu.
7	Bookmarks (C twice deletes one).
9	Bookmark this page.
Back	Back a page; on the first page, close the browser.

# Tabs (up to 5)
5 held	A new tab (type its address).
4, 6 held	The tab before or after.
C held	Close this tab (the last one closes the browser).
- With two tabs or more, their numbers show at the bar's right: tap one to switch, + for a new tab. The menu (*) lists them too.
- Links that open a new window open a new tab while there is room.
- The tabs come back next time. When memory runs short, the tab out of view longest is put away and loads again when you return to it.

# Touch
- Drag to scroll (it keeps gliding), pinch to zoom, tap to follow a link.

# Typing
- A text field on a page, or the address bar, switches the keypad to T9. Back leaves the field.

# The menu
- Address, bookmarks, reload, text size (Left and Right), ad blocking on or off, desktop site, home page, clear cookies, close.
- Ads are blocked with EasyList and uBlock Origin's filters, updated weekly. YouTube's ads are taken out of its videos too.
- With the lid closed, a video's sound goes on: the outer screen shows it, and the volume keys and the side key (twice: play or pause) control it.
- Downloads go to Downloads, with a notification when done.
