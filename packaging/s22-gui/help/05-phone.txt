Phone
Left, Right	The tabs: keypad, recent calls, contacts.

# Keypad
Digits	Type a number. Matching contacts show below it, by number or by the letters on the keys (5-6-2 finds "Jo..."); Up and Down pick one.
Call, OK	Call.
1 held	Voicemail (on an empty keypad).
2 to 9 held	That key's speed dial (set in Contacts).
C	Delete a digit.
- Each key beeps its tone at the media volume, unless sounds are off.

# Recent calls and contacts
Call, OK	Call.
#	Write a text instead (Messages).
*	On a recent call: save the number to Contacts, or show its contact.

# A ringing call
Call, OK	Answer.
Back, C	Decline.
Volume	Silence the ringing.
- Music and other sound turn down while it rings.

# During a call
Digits	Tones (for menus and voicemail); you hear each one too.
OK	Mute or unmute.
Right, speaker key	Speakerphone on or off.
Volume	Call volume.
Back	Hide the call screen; the call goes on, and Call brings it back.
End, lid	Hang up.
- Holding the phone to your ear turns the screen off and ignores touches.
- Other sound stops when a call starts (music pauses) and comes back when it ends.
