Camera
OK	Focus (when automatic) and take the photo.
*	Focus only.
#	Switch between the rear and front cameras.
Left, Right	Choose a setting in the bar: exposure, white balance, focus, flash.
Up, Down	Change that setting.
Back	Close the camera.
- Photos are saved to Pictures/Camera.
- The front camera has fixed focus and no flash, and its view is a mirror.
- Colours can come out a little red for now.
