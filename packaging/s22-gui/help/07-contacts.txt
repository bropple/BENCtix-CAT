Contacts
Digits	Search: the letters on the keys, or part of a number. C takes one back.
OK	Open a contact.
- The first rows make a new contact, or import a .vcf file from the home folder or Downloads (numbers already saved are skipped).

# A contact
OK on a number	Call it.
#	Text it.
*	Change it.
C, twice	Remove that number.
- Rows below: speed dials (keys 2 to 9 held in Phone), ringtone, text tone, call and text vibration, rename, add a number, and delete (press twice).
- Ringtones and vibrations play as you choose them.
