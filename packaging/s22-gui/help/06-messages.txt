Messages
Up, Down	Choose a conversation (newest first; unread ones are counted).
OK	Open it.
Call	Call that person.
*	Save the number to Contacts, or show its contact.
Back	Close Messages.
- The first row starts a new message: it asks for the number first.

# In a conversation
Keypad	Type a reply (see Typing).
OK	Send.
Back	Back to the list.
- Opening a conversation marks it read and clears its notifications.
- Pictures and group messages (MMS) do not work yet.
